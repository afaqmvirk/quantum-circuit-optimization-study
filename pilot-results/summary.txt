The pilot compared GPT-5.6-Sol, Qwen3-Coder 30B, and tket on 12 circuits.
On the Qiskit level-3 inputs, the starting circuits used 159 CX gates in total.
GPT finished with 118, tket with 153, and Qwen with 159. GPT beat tket on
three Cuccaro adders and tied it on the other nine circuits.

results.json contains the baseline counts and one result per model, circuit,
and starting condition. Each effective CX count retains the starting circuit
if no verified submission improves it. The raw condition starts from a circuit
translated to the common gate set. The qiskit3 condition starts after Qiskit
level-3 optimization.

cases.json contains the circuits. manifest.json records the prompt, model
settings, and software versions. The model folders contain individual attempts.
audit.json records the verification replay and paired comparisons.
